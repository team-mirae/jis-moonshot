# Common Japanese words in kana, for JIS Moonshot's kana reading (docs/moonshot.md §5).
# Hand-written for the moonshot: everyday words as kana-only typing spells them (no kanji
# conversion), loanwords in katakana. One word per line, optionally `word<TAB>count`; a word
# without a count counts as a common one. `#!particles` lines list particles, endings and
# auxiliaries that join words (Japanese is written without spaces, so a staged segment is often a
# phrase and is scored by how much of it known words and these cover).
