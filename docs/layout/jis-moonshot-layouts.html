Syllables with ㅇ
A legend with ㅇ (왜, 예, and Shift+vowel: 아, 애) is a real ㅇ, so it starts its own syllable: 말 + 왜 → 말왜. Bare jamo (ㅙ) join the consonant before them.
Bare vowels
Written without ㅇ (ㅙ ㅖ ㅢ): they join the consonant before them, ㄷ + ㅙ → 돼, and take the usual implied ㅇ with nothing before them.
Shift on the letter keys
Shift on a consonant types it with ㅏ: ⇧ㄱ → 가 (ㅃ ㅉ ㄸ ㄲ ㅆ are on 1–5). Shift on every vowel key, O and P too, starts a syllable on ㅇ (ㅒ ㅖ are on : and ;).
Symbols
^ ¥ @ [ ] . / \ type their JIS symbols, alone and with Shift; Korean keeps , on Shift. Predictions offer each mark's variants (。…、・), · for ¥, and a comma for ㅢ after an ending (그렇고,). ⌥ , . / [ ] type 、。・『』 (staged). 半角/全角 makes what's staged (or the word at the caret) full-width, Shift half-width.
F6 ひらがな · F7 カタカナ
The staged keys read as kana until the next commit. F8 (or Shift+F8) puts the kana reading's kanji on top.
F9 한글 · F10 ABC
F9 forces the Korean reading (as 変換; Shift+F9 bare, no implied ㅇ). F10 shows the staged keys as letters; again cycles UPPER → Capitalized. Neither commits.
かな · 英数 · Space
かな / 変換: to Mode A, or force Korean. 英数 / 無変換: commit and go to English. Space stages a space, so the words are read together; Shift+Space, Enter and Shift+Enter commit.
